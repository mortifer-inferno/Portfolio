My story

Hi, I’m Affan.

Online, I go by MORTIFER. I build websites from a completely blank page: the layout, the interactions, the details and the launch. Every project starts with one question: what should this feel like the moment someone lands on it?

So far that has meant a quiet luxury fashion store, a chauffeured travel service across Kerala, and a language studio that helps students prepare for IELTS and study abroad. Three very different businesses, and each one got its own look, its own voice and its own tools.

I care about the parts people feel more than the parts they see: how a page loads, how a button responds, how a section appears as you scroll. That is where a website stops being a page and starts being an experience.

I’m always learning, always shipping, and always looking for the next blank page.
